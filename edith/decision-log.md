# Decision Log

This file records approved product and technical decisions, rejected alternatives, assumptions, owners (if known), and unresolved questions.

## Format
Each decision entry should include:
- **Decision ID**: e.g., `D-001`, `D-002`
- **Date**: YYYY‑MM‑DD
- **Decision**: Statement of what was decided.
- **Rationale**: Brief explanation of why.
- **Alternatives Considered**: Other options that were evaluated.
- **Assumptions**: Any assumptions made.
- **Owner**: Person responsible (if applicable).
- **Unresolved Questions**: Any follow‑up items.
- **Status**: `Approved`, `Rejected`, `Pending`.
