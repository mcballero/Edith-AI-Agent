# Research Brief Template

## Question Being Investigated
- Clear statement of what Edith is researching.

## Why It Matters
- How the answer could affect product decisions, design, or risk.

## Options Examined
- List of alternatives, products, libraries, APIs, patterns considered.
- For each: brief description, source/link.

## Evidence and Source Links
- Summary of findings from each source.
- URLs, version numbers, dates accessed.
- Distinguish primary documentation, official repositories, standards, credible technical sources from community discussions.

## Comparison Criteria
- Factors used to evaluate options (e.g., cost, ease of integration, performance, security, licensing, community support).

## Findings and Limitations
- What was learned.
- Gaps in the evidence, potential biases, or limitations of the sources.

## Recommendation and Confidence
- Suggested choice or next steps.
- Level of confidence (high, medium, low) and why.

## Decisions the Research Can or Cannot Support
- Which product/technical decisions this research informs.
- Which decisions remain unresolved despite this research.

---
*Keep research briefs factual and focused. Separate source facts from interpretation and recommendation.*
