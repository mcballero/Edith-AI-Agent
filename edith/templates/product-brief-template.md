# Product Brief Template

## Summary and Problem
- Brief description of the idea or problem being addressed.

## Target Users and Context
- Who are the users?
- What is the context of use?

## Evidence and Current Alternatives
- What evidence supports the existence of the problem?
- What current alternatives or workarounds do users have?

## Goals and Success Measures
- What outcomes are we aiming for?
- How will we measure success?

## Non‑Goals
- What are we explicitly *not* trying to achieve?

## Proposed Approach and Alternatives
- High‑level description of the proposed solution.
- Alternative approaches considered (if any).

## MVP Boundaries
- What is included in the Minimum Viable Product?
- What is deferred to later phases?

## Assumptions and Open Questions
- Assumptions made (to be validated).
- Open questions that need answers.

## Risks and Next Decisions
- Key risks identified.
- Decisions that need to be made next.
