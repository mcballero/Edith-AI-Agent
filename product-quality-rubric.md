# Product Quality Rubric

## Criteria for a High‑Quality Product Artifact (e.g., Product Brief, PRD)

### 1. Problem & Evidence
- [ ] Problem statement is clear, specific, and user‑centered.
- [ ] Evidence supporting the problem is cited (user feedback, data, observations).
- [ ] Alternatives and current workarounds are identified.

### 2. Goals & Success Measures
- [ ] Goals are measurable and tied to business or user outcomes.
- [ ] Success metrics are defined and trackable.

### 3. Users & Journeys
- [ ] Primary user personas are described.
- [ ] Key user journeys are mapped and cover major goals.

### 4. Scope & Boundaries
- [ ] In‑scope and out‑of‑scope items are explicitly listed.
- [ ] MVP boundaries are justified and minimal.

### 5. Requirements Traceability
- [ ] Functional requirements have stable IDs.
- [ ] Each requirement links to user goals, evidence, or design.
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are testable and unambiguous.

### 6. Assumptions & Risks
- [ ] Assumptions are labeled and tracked.
- [ ] Key risks are identified with mitigation ideas.

### 7. Clarity & Conciseness
- [ ] Document is well‑structured and easy to navigate.
- [ ] Language is precise; avoids vague terms like “fast” or “easy” unless defined.

### 8. Non‑Functional Considerations
- [ ] Relevant performance, security, privacy, accessibility, reliability aspects are noted.

**Scoring**: Each criterion met = 1 point. Total score indicates readiness for engineering review.

---
*Use this rubric to review product briefs, PRDs, and similar artifacts.*
